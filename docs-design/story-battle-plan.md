# Samuright — Story and Battle Plan

## Core premise

Ren travels the Five Roads to earn five crests and become a Master Samurai. At the River Crossing he meets Hana, a cheerful and clumsy traveller who claims to be a commoner. She is Princess Hanae, fleeing an arranged marriage to Takamine Harunobu and carrying evidence that Lord Takamine Nobumasa has manufactured unrest to justify permanent rule.

Every story chapter ends in a word-slashing duel. Correct answers pressure the opponent's Resolve; incorrect or missed answers reduce Ren's Focus. Friendly masters test Ren rather than take physical damage.

## Modes

- **Story Mode:** authored dialogue, chapter vocabulary, named opponents, Focus, Resolve, master mechanics and crest rewards.
- **Dojo Mode:** two-minute score training, three lives, increasing speed, random vocabulary from the selected JLPT level, review and high-score replay.

## The Five Masters

| Crest | Master | First identity | Trial rule | Ability earned |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discipline | Iwao Jūbei | Old wandering ronin | Maintain clean combos amid disruption | Still Mind: combo briefly slows targets |
| Perception | Lady Shizuru | Foxfire Innkeeper | Read through convincing decoys | True Sight: a brief opening shimmer |
| Compassion | Genzō Masatsugu | Farmer Gen | Protect civilians and supplies | Guardian Heart: forgive one miss |
| Courage | Akane Tomoe | Crimson Traveller | Interrupt attacks under pressure | Rising Spirit: low-Focus comeback boost |
| Wisdom | Takamine Harunobu | Haru, a travelling retainer | Recognize when not to strike | Empty Blade: hold to calm the field |

## Chapter roadmap

1. **The Eastern Road — Iwao Jūbei:** tutorial duel. Jūbei challenges Ren to practise and quietly judges his potential. His identity as the First Master is revealed later at the Bamboo Dojo.
2. **The River Crossing — Saburō:** Ren meets Hana and protects her from the self-styled River Wolf. Hana first slips into royal speech.
3. **The Lantern Town — Mizuno Kichirō:** the honorable road captain searches for a missing princess and believes Ren may be her kidnapper.
4. **The Bamboo Dojo — Master Jūbei:** Discipline trial and first crest.
5. **The Foxfire Inn — Lady Shizuru:** Perception trial. Harunobu first appears as Haru.
6. **The Village Without a Lord — Genzō:** Compassion trial. Hana uses her knowledge of law to help ordinary people.
7. **The Crimson Pass — Akane:** Courage trial. Ren chooses Hana over the safe road.
8. **White Heron Castle — Harunobu:** the Fifth Master and Hana's identity are revealed together. Ren earns Wisdom by refusing a reckless strike.
9. **The Broken Alliance — Masanori:** Ren and Hana rebuild trust and decode the evidence in her pouch.
10. **The Golden Dojo — Nobumasa:** Ren rejects a false title founded on obedience and helps Hana expose the conspiracy.
11. **The Road Home — Jūbei:** a friendly final rematch confirms that Ren now understands mastery as responsibility.

## Battle presentation

- Compact opponent portrait and dialogue bubble at the upper-right edge.
- Ren remains the only fully animated combatant during the first production pass.
- Opponents react through portrait dialogue, Resolve changes, hit flashes, sound, particles and restrained screen movement.
- Dialogue bubbles appear at the opening, first mistake, combo milestone, phase change and victory; they must never cover answer targets.
- Later production can add expression portraits without requiring complete enemy sprite sheets.
